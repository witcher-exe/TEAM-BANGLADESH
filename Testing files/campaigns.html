How a campaign comes to life

From a meeting to a movement.

Step 1

Listen

Field surveys with local committees identify urgent needs.

Step 2

Plan

Resources, volunteers and timelines are mapped per district.

Step 3

Act

Mega-events, daily ops and partner orgs deploy on the ground.

Step 4

Measure

Public dashboards track outcomes & accountability.
