Mission and Vision

Paribesh Unnayan Sangstha (PUS)

Established in 2002 | Registered under the Bangladesh NGO Affairs Bureau

Our Vision

🌍 Looking Forward

Our Vision

To build a green, pollution-free, livable, and humane Bangladesh. We envision a world where a sustainable environment and a healthy, safe life are guaranteed for all—leaving behind a thriving, secure planet for future generations.

Our Mission

🎯 What We Do

Our Mission

To actively drive environmental conservation, sustainable urban development, and social welfare through dedicated, community-focused initiatives. We are committed to combating climate change, protecting natural resources, and empowering marginalized communities through accessible education and essential healthcare.

🌱 Domains of Impact

Core Areas of Focus

Our comprehensive initiatives are designed to bridge environmental sustainability with human welfare. We actively work across the following domains:

Environmental Conservation

Tree plantation and biodiversity preservation.

Urban Sustainability

Driving "Green City – Clean City" initiatives and pioneering eco-friendly housing projects.

Resource Management

Championing waste removal, recycling, alongside river, water, and sanitation development.

Climate Action

Implementing climate change mitigation strategies, disaster management, and air pollution prevention programs.

Social Empowerment

Ensuring education for street children and working toward the broader improvement of national education standards.

Public Health

Providing free medical services and vital healthcare to underprivileged and vulnerable populations.

"A pollution-free green earth, a healthy life."

🤝 Our Commitment

Guided by unyielding faith and collective effort, we are striving to deliver a sustainable, safe, and advanced future. By maintaining high professional standards, fostering new initiatives, and working as a unified team, we can turn our shared goals into reality.

Let’s build a greener, cleaner and healthier Bangladesh — together.
