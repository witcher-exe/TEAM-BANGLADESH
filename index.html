Who We Are

Paribesh Unnayan Sangstha

Registered under Social Welfare of Bangladesh

Paribesh Unnayan Sangstha is a reputed, registered NGO working for a long time with the goal of building a green, pollution-free, clean, livable, developed, and humane Bangladesh.

We work together with this pure belief - for the noble purpose of gifting a sustainable, safe, and better future for the next generations.
